# Control Crosswalk

Six vulnerability-management controls are assessed from evidence in the data (scanner, inventory, tickets, exceptions). Each control is cross-referenced to the frameworks below.

| Control | NIST CSF 2.0 | NIST 800-53 (RMF) | ISO 27001:2022 | PCI DSS v4.0 | HIPAA | CMMC / 800-171 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asset inventory is complete and owned | ID.AM-01, ID.AM-02 | CM-8 | A.5.9 | 12.5.1 | 164.310(d)(1) | 3.4.1 |
| Scanning covers in-scope assets | ID.RA-01, DE.CM-09 | RA-5 | A.8.8 | 11.3.1 | 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A) | 3.11.2 |
| Vulnerabilities remediated within SLA | PR.PS-02, ID.RA-06 | SI-2 | A.8.8 | 6.3.3 | 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(B) | 3.11.3, 3.14.1 |
| Known exploited vulnerabilities prioritized | ID.RA-02, ID.RA-05 | RA-5(2), SI-5 | A.5.7, A.8.8 | 6.3.1 | 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(B) | 3.14.3 |
| Remediation validated by rescan | ID.RA-01, ID.IM-02 | SI-2, CA-7 | A.8.8 | 11.3.1.3 | 164.308(a)(8) | 3.12.3 |
| Risk exceptions documented and current | ID.RA-06, GV.RM-04 | RA-7, PM-9 | Clause 6.1.3 | 12.3.1 | 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(B) | 3.12.2 |

> This crosswalk is a working reference for discussion, not an authoritative mapping.
